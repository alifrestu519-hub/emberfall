ECHOLOOP — Fight Beside Your Past Selves: a top-down arena-survival game. Survive six waves, an elite rogue cog, and a two-phase boss — on one of three windings (Gentle, Clockkeeper, Brutal), each with its own best score. Every cleared wave leaves an echo of yourself that fights beside you; Space rewinds body and health three seconds back; standing near an echo winds the paradox meter until time itself stops. Rust the clockwork foes until they seize, then break them apart — and replay any clock from its seed (`?seed=N`, or S after a run ends).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
